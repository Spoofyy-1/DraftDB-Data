All 50 statistics were evaluated against three within-cohort shuffled-value controls using three model seeds and fixed base-column selection: 603 completed tasks, no errors. Real and control columns have identical positions, missing-data patterns and marginal distributions. The earlier 270-run screen was confounded by added columns and is retained as exploratory history.

Gates require at least 0.5 percentage-point mean improvement over matched controls, improvements in at least two of three development classes, and no class regression exceeding one point. These are exploratory gates, not statistical significance or test achievement. The small number of historical classes limits confidence.
